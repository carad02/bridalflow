The experience

Your morning, with room to breathe.

Private yoga that slips naturally into your wedding preparations — made to feel personal, calm and completely unforced.

A softer rhythm

It’s not about the perfect pose.

It is about stepping away from the checklist, moving gently and sharing a moment with people you love.

We agree the best time with you beforehand, taking account of breakfast, getting-ready schedules, hair and makeup and travel to your ceremony. A short session might happen just after waking; a longer gathering could become the first shared moment of the day.

Each experience can weave in accessible movement, breathing, strength and mobility-inspired elements, and an unhurried closing rest.

A sample 60-minute session

From the first breath to the last.

10 min — Arrive, settle and breathe
35 min — Adaptable gentle flow and movement
10 min — Rest, grounding and quiet connection
5 min — A shared closing moment

This is a sample, not a fixed formula. A quieter or more playful practice is entirely possible.
