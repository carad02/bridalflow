Frequently asked questions

Everything you might be wondering.

The practical details, thoughtfully handled — so your morning feels beautifully simple.

Do I need to have done yoga before?

Not at all. The session is designed around your group, with gentle movement and options for beginners. You can choose a slower, more restorative experience instead of a flowing practice.

When should we schedule the session?

Most couples prefer a session before hair and makeup begins, or in a calm window early in the morning. We will check your ceremony and preparation timetable before confirming a start time.

How much space do we need?

Allow a clear, safe space roughly equivalent to one yoga mat per person, with room to move around each mat. We will discuss the room, floor surface and indoor alternative before booking.

Do you bring yoga mats and props?

Equipment arrangements are confirmed in the quote. We can discuss bringing a limited number of mats and simple props; larger groups or distant venues may incur an equipment fee. Please do not assume all equipment is included until confirmed.

Can people with different abilities join?

Yes. We aim to welcome mixed-ability groups and can offer seated or gentler alternatives where appropriate. Please discuss access and any relevant safety needs privately before confirming; we do not offer clinical treatment.

Can we practise outdoors?

Yes, when weather, privacy and ground conditions allow. An appropriate indoor alternative is required in the event of rain, heat or unsuitable terrain.

What locations do you cover?

Bristol is the main service area, with Bath, Somerset, Gloucestershire and the Cotswolds available by arrangement. Travel arrangements and any costs are agreed in your quote.

Can we book for more than 12 people?

Possibly. Larger groups need an individual quote and a safety and space assessment; a second instructor may be required.

Is a deposit required?

Any deposit and payment schedule will be set out in your written quote before you choose to book.

What if we need to cancel or change the date?

Cancellation, rescheduling and refund arrangements will be confirmed in your written agreement before you book.
